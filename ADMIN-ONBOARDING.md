# Staff onboarding: the admin side

Admin address: **https://<your site>/admin/** (staff sign in with email and password).

Everyone in the staff list can open **every admin screen**. **Owners** can also add and remove staff, change Shop settings, and delete titles, Pages text, Media images and series (regular staff can add and edit these, and can hide a title instead of deleting it). Every change staff make is recorded under Staff → Change log (owners only). Only add people you trust with customer names, phone numbers and emails.

## What you need before you start

- [ ] The new person's **name** (shown in change logs, e.g. "changed by Chris") and **email address**. Use an address that isn't already a customer account.
- [ ] You must be an **owner** (owners can manage staff; everyone else can only change their own password)

## Part 1: Add a staff member (owner, about 1 minute)

1. Sign in to the admin and open **Staff** (last item in the menu).
2. Under **Add a Staff Member**, enter their name and email. Leave the password blank to have one generated, or type one (10+ characters).
3. Click **Add staff member**. A box shows their sign-in address, email and password **once**. Click **Copy email and password** and send it to them privately (in person, or a message only they can see).

That's it: they can sign in right away. If you close the box before copying, use **Reset password** on their row to make a new one.

**Forgot your password?** Anyone on staff can reset their own: on the admin sign-in, type your email and click **Forgot your password?**, then follow the link in the email to choose a new one.

## Part 2: Their first sign-in (new staff member)

1. Go to https://<your site>/admin/ and sign in with the email and password you were given.
2. Go to **Staff → My Account** and change the starting password to your own (10+ characters).
3. If a screen looks out of date or something is missing after we've made a change, do a **hard refresh** (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows).
4. Work through the walk-through below, in this order.
5. **Sign out** (top right) when you leave a shared computer.

## House rules

- **Customer information is private.** Names, phones, emails and notes stay in the admin; don't paste them into chats or screenshots.
- **Shop settings:** only one person edits at a time. Saving overwrites that page, so if two people have it open, the last save wins.
- **Imports:** incentive and "N COPY" covers are never added. Release sheets list what comes out; future ordered lists are for ordering. Wednesday is new-release day.
- **Emails to everyone:** never use "Send to everyone now" to test. Use **Send a test to me**.
- **Deleting a customer cannot be undone** (it removes their profile, pull list, orders, follows, notes and login). Check twice.
- **Never share your login.** Ask the owner to add each person instead.

## Importing pull lists

For a shop moving to Pullwise: **Admin → Import → Pull Lists** brings in every customer and the series they take
from one spreadsheet (.xlsx, .xls or .csv). Owners only.

1. **Get the sheet ready.** Any of three layouts works: one row per customer and title; one row per customer with all
   their titles in one cell (one per line, or separated by commas or semicolons); or one sheet per customer, named
   after them. **Download sample CSV** shows the columns: customer name (or first and last name), email, phone, title,
   quantity, notes, Mature OK and No email. "Saga x2" in a title is read as 2 copies.
2. **Check the columns.** The import guesses the layout and which column is which; correct any guess, and watch the
   count of customers and titles change.
3. **Match titles.** Every title is matched to a series. **Matched** lines are ready. **Needs a look** lines show the
   closest series: pick one, or Skip. **Not found** lines: search for the series, **Add to series catalog** if it's
   new, or Skip. Every choice is remembered, so the same spelling matches by itself in later imports.
4. **Check the customers.** Each one is marked **New** or **In Pullwise** (same email, or same phone). Customers with
   no email and no phone are allowed but flagged: they can't be matched to an existing record, so make sure they're
   not already in Pullwise, and their pull list fills only once a phone or email is added.
5. **Save.** Nothing is saved until you press Save, and you can leave a review and resume it from **Drafts**.
   Saving adds each customer's follows and puts the titles open for pre-order now on their pull list (mature titles
   only for Mature OK customers); later titles arrive with the hourly update. Customers already in Pullwise keep what
   they have: their details are only filled in where blank, follows they already had stay as they were, and sheet notes
   are added under their existing notes. If a customer can't be saved, the reason shows beside them: fix it and press
   **Retry**; the others aren't redone. **Saving sends no email.** New customers get the weekly and arrival emails
   from then on unless the sheet says No email.

**Download skipped and not found** gives a CSV of the lines left out, to follow up by hand. A record of each import is
in **Recent Runs**; the review's details are deleted 30 days after it is saved or discarded.

## Part 4: Changing or removing access (owner)

All of this is on **Admin → Staff**, in the "Staff List" table:

- **Forgot a password:** click **Reset password** on their row. A new one is shown once; send it privately.
- **Make someone an owner** (or back to regular staff): **Make owner** / **Make staff**. Owners can add and remove staff.
- **Someone leaves:** click **Remove** on their row. They are locked out of every admin screen right away, and their login is deleted (unless the same email is also a customer account, which stays).
- **Guard rails:** you can't remove yourself, and there always has to be at least one owner.
- **Super Admin:** one account sits above Owner. A Super Admin can't be removed or demoted, and an owner can't reset their password; the buttons simply aren't shown on their row. Only the database can add or retire a Super Admin (see the note at the top of `supabase/migrations/20270102000000_super_admin.sql`).
- **See who has access and when they last signed in:** the same table.
